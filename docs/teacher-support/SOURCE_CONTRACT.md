# CAM Primary · MS1 Source Contract

Phase 0 registers source identity and authority only. It does not ship criterion text, classroom evidence, checklists, comparisons, or subject examples.

- `cam-primary-ms1-official` is the canonical/master source: **MS1 Report Teacher Guidance**.
- `cam-primary-ms1-interpretive` is the classroom interpretation/example source: **MS1 All Levels Complete Bilingual**.
- Interpretive material may clarify the official source, but must not replace it, broaden it, contradict it, or create score cut-offs that the official source does not define.
- Future plain-language explanations, observation checklists, classroom evidence, comparisons, and subject examples must trace to a parent official criterion before they can be enabled.
- If the two sources conflict, the canonical official source wins.
